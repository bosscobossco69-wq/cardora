What information we collect

When available, account registration and marketplace services may collect your email address, name, login identifiers, seller application details, shipping/contact information and interactions with listings or orders. Do not submit identity documents unless Cardora provides a verified secure verification flow.

Why we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, review seller/agent applications, moderate listings, administer seller subscriptions, prevent abuse, support transactions and respond to customer requests. Only features actually enabled on the website collect corresponding data.

Payment information

When Cardora's paid seller subscriptions are enabled, Stripe handles the hosted payment page and payment method details. Cardora expects to receive limited billing records such as subscription identifiers, plan, payment result and invoice references. Cardora should not receive or store full card numbers in its website database. Test transactions in Stripe Sandbox are not real payments.

Hosting and providers

Cardora uses or plans to use web hosting, Supabase for account/database services and Stripe for subscriptions. These providers may process data in other jurisdictions under their own service terms. Other integrations must be disclosed as they are enabled.

Sharing and retention

Account data and application information should only be visible to authorized staff or the owning user, subject to access controls. We may retain information needed for legitimate account, dispute, security, accounting and legal purposes, and remove or anonymize it when no longer needed. A precise retention schedule must be approved before launch.

Your choices and requests

You can request access, correction or deletion where applicable, or contact Cardora about suspected misuse of your personal information. Legal requirements may limit deletion of billing or dispute records.

Cookies and authentication

The website may use local browser storage and cookies to maintain sessions and keep essential preferences. Additional analytics or advertising uses must be disclosed when enabled.
