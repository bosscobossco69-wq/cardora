Which collectible cards can sellers apply with?

Applications are open in principle to collectors working with genuine trading cards in several categories. Acceptance of a seller application is not approval to publish any card type: each category requires its own verification and moderation support before listings go live.

Sports and other collectible cards

Not accepted: counterfeits sold as genuine, stolen or unowned stock, misleading photos, unauthorized brand claims, or products the seller cannot lawfully fulfill. Fan-made cards must never be represented as official.

Seller or referral agent?

Sellers may apply without owning cards today; publishing requires real inventory, truthful photos and approval. Referral agents do not need cards, but may only promote Cardora truthfully; commissions, referral points and cash rewards are NOT active or guaranteed.
