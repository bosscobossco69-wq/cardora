4What "not active" means

  • No payment provider is connected, so Trixovel cannot take a card, bank, FPX or wallet payment.
  • No invoice, receipt or payout can be issued by Trixovel.
  • No commission is owed, payable or reserved. A seller keeps everything agreed with the buyer.
  • Do not send money to anyone claiming to collect a Trixovel fee or to hold funds for Trixovel.
  • Refund and chargeback rules will be published before any payment is accepted.

5How a future fee would be disclosed

Before any payment is taken, the checkout would have to show: the charge currency, the exchange-rate source and timestamp, the item price, the commission, shipping, any tax, and the final amount. Amounts in different currencies are different amounts.

Any change to this page is published here with an effective date before it applies. No fee applies retroactively to a listing or a sale that happened earlier.

7Questions

Does Trixovel charge any fee today?

No. The current fee schedule is 0%. There is no listing fee, no transaction fee, no commission and no subscription. Buyers pay Trixovel nothing and a seller keeps the full amount agreed with the buyer.

When would the planned 5% seller commission start?

Only after a payment provider is connected and this page shows an effective date for that fee. No start date is promised today. Until then the commission is zero and nothing is owed or reserved.

Do buyers pay a Trixovel fee?

No buyer fee is planned. The proposed commission is charged to the seller. Any future buyer charge would have to be shown at checkout before a payment is made.

How would the agent referral share be paid?

The proposed share is 25% of the platform commission, paid by Trixovel out of its own share, never added to the buyer price. The agent program is not active and no payout can be claimed.

Are there listing or subscription fees?

No listing fee is planned for the first 100 listings and no subscription is planned. Featured or pinned placement would be optional and paid only if a seller chooses it.

What about refunds and chargebacks?

Rules will be published before any payment is accepted. Today Trixovel operates no payment system and holds no funds, so any purchase is settled directly between buyer and seller, and Trixovel cannot recover money paid to a seller.

Is Trixovel a registered company?

Not yet. Trixovel is operated by an individual as a personal project in Malaysia. If a business registration is completed, this page is updated before any fee is ever charged.
