4What "not active" means

  • ToyyibPay merchant approval is confirmed, but Cardora website checkout is not yet live while integration and testing are completed.
  • Do not rely on a return URL alone as proof of payment; Cardora must verify the server callback and transaction status.
  • No commission is owed, payable or reserved. A seller keeps everything agreed with the buyer.
  • Do not send money to anyone claiming to collect a Cardora fee or to hold funds for Cardora.
  • Refund and chargeback rules will be published before any payment is accepted.

5How a future fee would be disclosed

Before any payment is taken, the checkout would have to show: the charge currency, the exchange-rate source and timestamp, the item price, the commission, shipping, any tax, and the final amount. Amounts in different currencies are different amounts.

Any change to this page is published here with an effective date before it applies. No fee applies retroactively to a listing or a sale that happened earlier.

7Questions

Does Cardora charge any fee today?

No. The current fee schedule is 0%. Cardora's current platform commission is 0%. Seller subscription fees are separate and are shown in the plan table.

When would the future 1% platform commission start?

Only after Cardora publishes an effective date and updates checkout disclosures. Until then platform commission remains 0%.

Do buyers pay a Cardora fee?

No buyer-side Cardora service fee is currently specified. Any future charge must be disclosed at checkout before payment.

How would the agent referral share be paid?

Agent referral reward is 25% of actual platform commission, paid from Cardora's commission share. With current commission at 0%, referral reward is 0.

What are the Seller subscription plans?

Free RM0 for 5 listings; Basic RM29 for 30 listings; Pro RM79 for 150 listings; Business RM199 for 600 listings. Paid plan activation requires verified payment. Trial and grace periods are 7 days each.

What about refunds and chargebacks?

Refund, dispute and payout rules will be published before live payments are enabled. Do not rely on a browser return URL alone as proof of payment.

Is Cardora a registered company?

Cardora's ToyyibPay merchant account has been approved. Website checkout remains disabled until integration and end-to-end verification are complete.
