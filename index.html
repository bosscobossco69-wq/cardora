Cardora Trust, Privacy & Marketplace Policies

Plain-language draft for the preview. The final legal entity/contact details, service provider, country coverage, data-retention periods and fee schedule must be completed before launch.

Preview only: Cardora has not enabled real payments, custody of funds, escrow, refunds, or seller payouts. This page is not proof of payment or a legal guarantee.

1. Privacy & personal data

Cardora should collect only information needed to operate accounts and listings, match buyers and sellers, fulfil orders, provide support, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Depending on enabled features, this may include account/contact details, listing content, messages and reports, shipping details, order/payment references, device/security logs and seller verification status.

  • Do not store full card numbers, bank login credentials, passwords or one-time passcodes in Cardora. Payment details should be handled by an approved provider.
  • Limit access by role; use server-side authorization, database row-level security, encrypted transport, secret management, audit logs, backups and retention/deletion rules.
  • Share only necessary order/shipping details with the relevant transaction party, payment provider, carrier or service provider. Cross-border transfers must be reviewed and disclosed where required.
  • Use data only for disclosed purposes and applicable legal requirements. Any analytics, marketing cookies or optional profiling must be disclosed and consented to where required.
  • Before launch, publish the real operator identity and contact, purposes, recipients, retention periods, user rights and a working privacy-request channel.

Users should not upload identity documents or sensitive financial information to public listing descriptions or chat. If identity verification is required, it must use a controlled, access-restricted workflow.

2. Marketplace role & seller responsibility

Cardora is intended to connect independent third-party sellers and buyers. Cardora does not itself own or sell each listed card. Sellers are responsible for lawful ownership, authenticity representations, accurate condition/photos, pricing, packaging, dispatch and truthful tracking information. Buyers must review the listing, shipping cost and final checkout total before placing an order.

Cardora may moderate listings, keep transaction records, investigate reports and coordinate disputes. This does not remove any mandatory consumer rights or legal responsibilities of Cardora, sellers or payment providers. The final terms must identify who is the contractual seller, who receives the buyer's payment, who issues invoices and who is responsible for shipping, taxes, cancellations and eligible refunds in each supported market.

3. Platform fees, commissions & price differences

Cardora may earn a disclosed transaction fee, seller commission, listing fee or other agreed platform fee. If the buyer-facing price differs from the seller's listed amount or seller proceeds, the price structure and all mandatory charges must be shown clearly before the buyer confirms the order and before the seller accepts the applicable fee terms.

All example percentages or calculations shown elsewhere in this preview are illustrative only. A production fee schedule must specify who pays each fee, how currency conversion is calculated, any payment-provider charge, taxes, rounding, chargebacks and when fees may be reversed. Do not describe a hidden markup as a bank transfer fee or claim a spread is an exchange rate.

4. Payment, refunds, disputes & seller payouts

  • Only a server-verified payment-provider event can mark an order paid. Screenshots, user input or a browser redirect are not proof of payment.
  • Cardora must not accept or hold customer money or manually redistribute funds through a personal bank account unless the relevant provider and legal framework expressly allow the chosen model.
  • Seller payouts require verified seller/payout details, eligibility checks, ledger reconciliation and provider confirmation.
  • Cardora is not promising “no refunds.” Seller policies, mandatory consumer law, payment-provider chargebacks and dispute outcomes may require cancellation, refund or other remedies. The final policy must explain the process and responsibilities.
  • Never ask users to share passwords, OTPs, bank-login credentials or full payment-card details with support. Report suspicious listings, impersonation, payment requests and account takeovers.

5. Access, correction, deletion & complaints

Before launch, Cardora must provide a real support/privacy contact and process for users to request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible personal data, subject to legal record-retention duties. Requests must be authenticated, logged, limited to the requester's data and completed within applicable deadlines. A breach-response plan and a method for reporting suspected account compromise are also required.

Draft for product planning, not legal advice. Review with a qualified Malaysian adviser and the chosen payment provider before enabling transactions.
