Trixovel Privacy, Marketplace Terms & Fees

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Contact: Trixovel Support — nkj30733838@gmail.com

Who runs Trixovel: an individual, as a personal project based in Malaysia. Trixovel is not a registered company, so there is no company name, registration number or invoice to show — and Trixovel does not take any money in the first place. If a business registration is obtained later, this page is updated before any fee is ever charged.

These terms describe Trixovel as it operates today: a free preview that displays collectible-card listings. Trixovel does not collect, hold, route or refund any payment. We do not sell cards ourselves. Every listing is offered by an independent third-party seller, and that seller — not Trixovel — is the party who sells, invoices, ships and refunds.

No money passes through Trixovel. Payments, wallets, deposits, escrow, refunds and seller payouts are not enabled. If anyone tells you to send money to a “Trixovel” bank account, QR code or e-wallet in order to buy, reserve or verify a card, that is not us — stop and report it from this site.

1. Privacy & personal data (Malaysia PDPA 2010 notice)

We collect only what is needed to run the site: your email address and display name when you create an account, the listings and messages you choose to publish, and standard security logs. Sign-in uses Google or email verification, so Google supplies us with your email address and profile name. Your data is stored with our database host, Supabase, which processes it on our behalf.

  • We never ask for, and never store, full payment-card numbers, bank login credentials, passwords or one-time passcodes. Do not send those to anyone, including anyone claiming to be “support”.
  • No advertising or marketing cookies are running today. If we add an ad network or analytics later, this section is updated first and a consent notice is shown.
  • Who can see your data: the other party in a transaction you take part in (name and shipping details only), our service providers, and anyone who views a listing you make public. Never put your phone number, home address or external account handles inside a public listing or chat.
  • Where it is stored: our database and hosting may be located outside Malaysia. By using Trixovel you acknowledge this notice; write to us if you need a copy or a correction.
  • Retention: account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Removed listings may persist briefly in backups or search caches.
  • Your rights: you may request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent by emailing the contact address at the top of this page. We may retain records where the law requires it.
  • Children: Trixovel is not directed at people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

2. Marketplace role & seller responsibility

Trixovel connects independent third-party sellers with buyers. Trixovel does not own the cards shown and is not the seller of anything on this site. The seller named on a listing is your contractual counterparty. That seller is responsible for lawful ownership, authenticity claims, condition and photo accuracy, pricing, packaging, dispatch and truthful tracking.

Where the money goes today: because Trixovel operates no payment system, any agreed purchase is settled directly between buyer and seller. Trixovel does not receive, hold or forward funds, and cannot issue a receipt or invoice on a seller's behalf. Paying outside Trixovel also means Trixovel cannot offer you purchase protection, so check the seller and the card details before you agree anything.

We may review listings, keep records, investigate reports and remove content that breaks our rules. That moderation does not transfer the seller's responsibility to us and does not remove any mandatory consumer rights you hold against the seller.

3. Platform fees, commissions & how Trixovel earns

Current fee schedule: 0%. Trixovel charges no listing fee, no transaction fee, no commission and no subscription. Buyers pay nothing to Trixovel, and a seller keeps the full amount agreed with the buyer.

We may introduce a disclosed seller commission, listing fee, featured-placement fee or subscription later. If we do: the schedule in this section is updated before any charge is applied, existing sellers are notified and may stop selling instead of accepting, every charge is shown on screen before it is accepted, and a fee on a cancelled or refunded order is reversed. We will never add an undisclosed markup to a price, and we will not describe a currency spread as a bank fee.

Any percentage or amount shown elsewhere on this site as an example is illustrative only until it appears in this section.

4. Refunds, disputes & payment safety

  • Refunds. Trixovel takes no money, so Trixovel issues no refunds. A refund, replacement or cancellation is agreed directly with the seller. Before paying anyone, ask that seller to state their refund terms in writing on the listing.
  • Disputes. Use the Report a User / Listing form. We can review evidence, remove listings and suspend accounts, but we cannot force a seller to refund, and we cannot recover money paid directly to a seller.
  • Proof of payment. A screenshot, a chat message or a browser redirect is not proof that money was sent. Never release a card on a screenshot alone.
  • What we will never ask for: your password, a one-time code (OTP), your bank login, or a full payment-card number. Anyone asking for those is an impostor.
  • Off-platform payment requests that tell you to pay a “Trixovel account”, or to move the deal to WhatsApp or Telegram to avoid a fee, are a scam signal — report them.
  • If Trixovel ever processes payments, this section will name the provider, state how an order is marked paid, the refund window, who bears a chargeback, and how and when a seller is paid out.

5. Data requests, complaints & account removal

Email the contact address at the top of this page from the same address you registered with, and say which you want: a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account and listings. We verify the request, log it, and act within a reasonable period. Deletion may not remove data we must keep by law, or messages another user has reported to us.

To report a scam listing, impersonation, a suspected counterfeit card, or an account you believe was taken over, use the Report form on this site.
